Privacy

Tilerun collects no personal data.

  • The game has no analytics, tracking, advertising, or cookies.
  • Your in-progress round, your daily completion, and your settings are stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes them.
  • If you are signed in and win a daily round, the score is sent to the FreeGameStore leaderboard service. Nothing else leaves your device.
  • The game asks for no device permissions. Sounds are off until you turn them on, and they follow the platform mute setting.

Questions: see freegamestore.online.
