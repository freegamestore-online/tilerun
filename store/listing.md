## Description

Tilerun is dominoes with a clean table. Every player gets a hand from the double-six set. Match a pip value at either end of the line to play, and be the first to empty your hand. When nobody can move, the round is blocked and the lowest total wins.

- Two game types: **Draw**, where you draw from the boneyard when you cannot play, and **Block**, where you pass instead.
- Play **vs AI** at Easy, Normal, or Hard, or **pass and play** with two to four players on one device.
- A **daily challenge** with the same deal for everyone on the same UTC date. Win it to post your score to the leaderboard.
- Your round is saved as you play, so a reload picks up where you left off.
- Light and dark mode, following your system setting. Animations turn off when reduced motion is on.
- Works offline once loaded. Installs to your home screen.
